A MESSAGE FROM SPACE

Leo is thirteen years old and loves space. He enjoys watching rocket launches, reading about planets and stars, and building small robots. His parents think it is an interesting hobby, but Leo dreams of working in space science one day. One summer, Leo visited an old radio observatory with his uncle. Most visitors wanted to look through the big telescope, but Leo was more interested in the large satellite dishes outside. A guide explained that these dishes could receive signals from space.

Suddenly, a computer made a strange noise. BEEP... BEEP... BEEP...

Everyone stopped talking. A message appeared on the screen: HELLO. IS ANYONE THERE?

The guide smiled. "It's probably a computer problem," he said. The adults left the room, but Leo stayed. A few seconds later, another message appeared: WE HAVE FOUND YOUR PLANET.

Leo looked around. Nobody else was in the room. He quickly took a photo of the screen with his phone. When he got home, he showed the photo to his parents. His dad laughed. "Someone is probably playing a joke," he said. His mum looked at the photo carefully. "I'm not so sure," she replied.

The next morning, Leo searched the internet for information about strange signals from space. He found several stories, but none of them were like the message in his photo. Two days later, the observatory called Leo's family. The scientists had checked all the computers, but they couldn't find the message.

Then something even stranger happened. The photo on Leo's phone changed! Now, it showed only an empty computer screen. Nobody knew why. Leo still keeps the phone in his bedroom. He is going to visit the observatory again next summer. Maybe he will receive another message. Or maybe someone in space is still trying to contact Earth...
